Who we are

Keiross Lifescience Pvt. Ltd.

Keiross Lifescience Private Limited is a pharmaceutical healthcare company engaged in marketing quality pharmaceutical formulations and healthcare products under its own brand names.

Our portfolio covers prescription medicines, pharmaceutical formulations and supportive nutraceutical products across key therapeutic categories. Every product is manufactured through approved, qualified pharmaceutical manufacturing partners.

  • Own-brand portfolio across multiple therapy areas
  • Tablets, capsules, syrups, suspensions and injections
  • Supplying distributors, stockists, pharmacies, hospitals and clinics
  • Focused on Jharkhand, with plans for pan-India expansion
