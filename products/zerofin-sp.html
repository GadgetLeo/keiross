Zerofin-SP

Don't let pain & inflammation play with health anymore

Each tablet contains
Aceclofenac 100 mg + Paracetamol 325 mg + Serratiopeptidase 15 mg

“…For effortless joint movement”

Prescription medicine — supplied to licensed trade buyers and for use under medical supervision only.

Key benefits

Aceclofenac

  • Produces preferential COX-2 blockade
  • Rapid onset of action
  • Stimulatory effects on cartilage matrix synthesis
  • Reduces pain intensity and inflammation and improves functional capacity of the knee

Paracetamol

  • Potent analgesic & antipyretic agent

Serratiopeptidase

  • Anti-inflammatory, anti-oedemic & fibrinolytic activity
  • Speeds up tissue repair and healing

Indications

  • Low back pain
  • Osteoarthritis
  • Rheumatoid arthritis
  • Fibrositis & tendonitis
  • Ankylosing spondylitis
  • Post-surgical / traumatic injury
