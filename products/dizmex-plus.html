Dizmex-Plus

Sometimes big bites lead to big problems

Composition
Alpha Galactosidase 25 mg + Pepsin 10 mg + Lipase 10 mg + Protease 3 mg + Lactase 25 mg

“Increases hunger and improves digestion”

Send Enquiry
Prescription medicine — supplied to licensed trade buyers and for use under medical supervision only.

Key benefits

Alpha Galactosidase

  • Helps digest the complex sugars found in beans, legumes and cruciferous vegetables such as cabbage, cauliflower and broccoli

Fungal Diastase

  • Breaks large insoluble starch molecules into soluble starches for better assimilation
  • Effective in diarrhoea, constipation, burps and belches
  • Eliminates the sense of fullness, nausea and vomiting after meals

Pepsin

  • Degrades proteins to oligopeptides & amino acids
  • Helps correct digestive disturbance

Lipase

  • Helps digest the fats in food

Indications

  • Constipation
  • Loss of appetite
  • Sense of fullness
