Respiratory & AllergySyrup℞ Prescription

Rossberry-LS

Ease the difficulty in breathing with a complete cough formulation

Composition
Levosalbutamol 1 mg + Ambroxol 30 mg + Guaiphenesin 50 mg per 5 ml

“Bronchodilator, mucolytic & expectorant”

Send Enquiry
Prescription medicine — supplied to licensed trade buyers and for use under medical supervision only.

Key benefits

Levosalbutamol

  • Safe and effective bronchodilator
  • Same therapeutic effect as salbutamol without tachycardia and hypokalaemia

Ambroxol

  • Promotes mucus clearance, facilitates expectoration and eases productive cough
  • Effective in reducing exacerbations of chronic bronchitis and in protection from inflammatory reactions

Guaiphenesin

  • US FDA-approved expectorant
  • Loosens phlegm and increases lubrication of the lungs, allowing a productive cough

Indications

  • Asthma
  • Bronchitis
  • Bronchospasm
  • Mouth or throat irritation
  • Chronic inflammatory pulmonary conditions
